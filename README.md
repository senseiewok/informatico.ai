# Informatico.ai — Living Executable Monographs (`60 Folios: 48 Pillar Folios · 6 Open Bounties · 6 Tech & Tools`)

**Live Bilingual Publication:** [https://informatico.ai/](https://informatico.ai/) · **Español (`ES-MX`):** [https://informatico.ai/es-mx/](https://informatico.ai/es-mx/)

`Informatico.ai` is an open-access, zero-build, bilingual (`English` + `Mexican Spanish`) scientific and engineering monograph series uniting **Civil Engineering & Construction (`E.01–E.12`)**, **Architecture (`A.01–A.12`)**, **Physics & Quantum (`P.01–P.12`)**, **Mathematics (`M.01–M.12`)**, **Open Bounties & Proofs (`B.01–B.06`)**, and **Tech & Tools · The Toolbox (`X.01–X.06`)**.

Every essay pairs:
1. **5-Pace Human Distillation Ladder & Primary Sources**: History first, followed immediately by an *"In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation"* bridge, a tactile everyday analogy, a contextual 3-panel Visual Intuition Bridge SVG (`Plate D`), and clickable DOIs verified offline against the Crossref registry (`data/folio-citations.cache.json`).
2. **Zero-Build Interactive SVG + WebGL2 Workbenches & 3D GLB Inspector**: 5-Layer declarative SVG (`document.createElementNS`), analytical Ray-AABB WebGL2 3D surfaces, a live zero-dependency WebGL2 `.glb` binary mesh inspector (`src/modules/glb-viewer.mjs`), and a **Counterfactual Axiom / Planted-Defect Knockout (`⚡`)** mode.
3. **Portable AI `SKILL.md` Companions, Neutral 3D/CAD Files & Verifiers That Can Fail**: The 48 pillar essays and 6 bounties ship plain-text `SKILL.md` companions whose reference values are stated in the essay. The Toolbox, Time & Gravity and cross-scale essay skills also ship verifiers that must fail under a planted defect. On 2026-11-13, 54 generated placeholder scripts were removed from `skills/` (48 pillar essays and 6 bounty skills). They stored each essay's values as text and printed PASS without computing anything. Those skills are now plain-text `SKILL.md` files, and `skills/verify_all_48_folios.py` now rejects any verifier that cannot fail. The earlier files remain in git history. All `SKILL.md` files (`Claude Code`, `GitHub Copilot`, `Cursor`, `Antigravity`, `Codex`, local `Ollama` models), Python generators (`make_x01.py..make_x06.py`), and those verifiers live under [`skills/`](skills/).
