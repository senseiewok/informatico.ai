# Neuro-Symbolic Deductive Search (AlphaGeometry 2): Auxiliary Constructions and Allosteric Pocket Triangulation (Folio M.05)

A plain-text companion to the essay at https://informatico.ai/math/m05/ (`M.05 · Synthetic & Distance Geometry`, `REV 1.2`, redated `2026-11-14`).

## The governing relation
`DD+AR Closure · Cayley-Menger det(B_5) = 0`

## Rules the essay derives
1. Saturate forward symbolic deduction (DD) and linear algebraic ratio/angle chasing (AR) before invoking neural auxiliary proposals.
2. Verify 3D Euclidean distance matrices via the Cayley-Menger determinant det(B_{n+1}) = 0 and alternating sign minors.
3. Say plainly when a geometric picture is only an analogy for a biological structure, and make no claim about how any drug works.

## How it works, in four stages
- Stage 1 (Premise Graph Encoding): Translates geometric incidences (collinearity, concyclicity, distance ratios) into a symbolic hypergraph G_0.
- Stage 2 (Symbolic DD+AR Fixed-Point Closure): Runs deductive database rules and algebraic angle/ratio/area chasing until saturation.
- Stage 3 (Neural Auxiliary Construction X_aux): Proposes the missing auxiliary point/circle that bridges disconnected deductive components.
- Stage 4 (Cayley-Menger Check): Five points in three-dimensional space must satisfy det(B_5) = 0 for their squared distances. The page uses this only to illustrate a rigidity condition; it is not a protein calculation.

## Recent research context
- **AlphaGeometry Reports 25 of 30 Olympiad Problems; AlphaGeometry2 Reports 84% of IMO Geometry 2000–2024**: Published in Nature by Trinh, Wu, Le, He & Luong (DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06747-5), AlphaGeometry pairs a symbolic deduction engine with a neural model, trained on about 100 million synthetic theorems, that proposes auxiliary constructions, and reports 25 of 30 olympiad-level test problems solved. A follow-up report on AlphaGeometry2 (arXiv:2502.03544) states an 84% solving rate on IMO geometry problems from 2000 to 2024. This site reports these results and did not reproduce them.
