I · Two Different Spaces, One Exact Index Theorem

Weld 12 steel rods with frictionless ball joints into the 12 edges of a 3D cube of V = 8 vertices, set it on a laboratory table, and push gently on an upper corner. Even if every rod is infinitely stiff in axial tension and compression, the frame folds effortlessly into a rhombohedral prism. Now step across physics to the tabletop quantum-gravity witness proposed independently by Bose et al. (2017) and Marletto and Vedral (2017): 2 diamond microspheres of mass M = 10⁻¹⁴ kg fall side by side at center separation d = 450 µm, each held in a 2-branch spatial superposition of width Δx = 250 µm for T = 2.5 s. Hand a mathematician only the abstract 4-dimensional complex Hilbert space ℂ⁴ of the 4 joint branches without specifying which operators belong to left trap A and which belong to right trap B. Just like the unbraced cube, the bare state vector can be continuously deformed by global unitary rotations in SU(4) that turn an entangled pair into a separable product state without altering a single eigenvalue of the density matrix.

Precision requires stating what is NOT shared before naming what is. Structural mechanics and quantum subsystem locality act on completely different vector spaces, and rigorous modeling never conflates spatial coordinates with Lie-algebra generators. A 3D pin-jointed truss with V ≥ 3 non-collinear joints lives in Euclidean configuration space ℝ³ⱽ; quotienting out the 6 rigid-body motions of se(3) (3 translations and 3 rotations) leaves an internal deformation space of dimension D_mech = 3V − 6 constrained by E axial bar-length equations. A 2-qubit quantum experiment at a fixed clock slice lives instead on ℋ_A ⊗ ℋ_B ≅ ℂ² ⊗ ℂ², where continuous basis refactorizations U = exp(i X) belong to the 15-dimensional Lie group SU(4), so the infinitesimal generator X lives in a real vector space of dimension D_alg = 15 constrained by E_alg = 54 commutator equations enforcing spatial microcausality. What unites both worlds is not a metaphor, but the exact rank-nullity theorem of linear algebra.

Plain-Language Bridge · Zero Modes and Redundant Locks

Why counting equations minus unknowns misses hidden hinges unless you check matrix rank

Every first-order constraint system maps D candidate motions to E checks via a linear operator C of rank r. The D − r nullspace directions (ker C) are zero modes deforming the system at 0 linear cost, while the E − r cokernel combinations (coker C = ker Cᵀ) are equilibrium states of self-stress at 0 external load or redundant Jacobi identities.

Subtracting cokernel from nullspace cancels rank r identically: (D − r) − (E − r) = D − E. Scalar subtraction D − E fixes the net index, yet only Gaussian elimination of r reveals whether zero modes and self-stresses both vanish or cancel in pairs.

Fundamental Rank–Nullity Index Identity dim ker(C) − dim coker(C) = (D − rank(C)) − (E − rank(C)) = D − E

For any real linear constraint operator C : ℝᴰ → ℝᴱ of rank r = rank(C), nullity dim ker(C) = D − r counts unconstrained zero modes while corank dim coker(C) = dim ker(Cᵀ) = E − r counts redundant constraint dependencies or equilibrium states of self-stress.

II · Space Frames and Tensegrity: Why Scalar Counting Needs Matrix Rank

In 1864 James Clerk Maxwell wrote down the bar-and-joint counting rule for stiff 3D frames: V joints carry 3V Cartesian coordinates, 6 of which move the whole frame as a rigid body in Euclidean space, leaving D_mech = 3V − 6 internal degrees of freedom to be locked by E bars. Over a century later, Calladine (1978) and Pellegrino and Calladine (1986) resolved why Buckminster Fuller's and Kenneth Snelson's cable-and-strut tensegrity sculptures stood taut even when Maxwell's scalar count predicted mechanisms. Projecting the E × 3V nodal compatibility matrix onto the (3V − 6)-dimensional orthogonal complement of se(3) produces the internal rigidity matrix R ∈ ℝᴱˣ⁽³ⱽ⁻⁶⁾, whose nullity is N_floppy = (3V − 6) − rank(R) and whose transpose nullity is N_self_stress = E − rank(R). Subtracting yields the Maxwell–Calladine theorem: N_floppy − N_self_stress = (3V − 6) − E.

Watch our pure-Python Gaussian-elimination solver track R across 3 canonical space frames. First, a regular convex octahedron has V = 6 vertices, D_mech = 3(6) − 6 = 12 internal degrees of freedom, and E = 12 triangular edges; its 12 × 12 internal rigidity matrix has full rank 12, so N_floppy = 0 and N_self_stress = 0 (strictly isostatic). Second, return to the V = 8 unit cube [0, 1]³, where D_mech = 3(8) − 6 = 18. With only its E = 12 axial edges, R ∈ ℝ¹²ˣ¹⁸ has rank 12, leaving N_floppy = 6 zero-energy modes (3 face shears plus 3 warping modes) and N_self_stress = 0. Adding 1 diagonal cross-brace across each of the 6 distinct faces raises rank(R) by 1 per brace—12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 16 → 17 → 18—driving N_floppy from 6 down to 0 at E = 18 with N_self_stress = 0. Inserting a 7th space diagonal across the body interior (E = 19) cannot raise rank(R) above 18, so N_floppy stays 0 while N_self_stress jumps to 1 (index 0 − 1 = 18 − 19 = −1).

Now test why scalar counting fails without rank(R). Keep V = 8 and E = 18 so (3V − 6) − E = 0, but place 2 diagonals on the z = 0 face while leaving z = 1 unbraced. A 2D 4-node square has 2(4) − 3 = 5 planar degrees of freedom, so 4 edges plus 2 diagonals (6 bars) create 1 planar self-stress; Gaussian elimination on the 3D cube gives rank(R) = 17, uncovering N_floppy = 1 shear mode paired with N_self_stress = 1 (1 − 1 = 0). Calladine (1978) showed tensegrity uses this rank drop constructively: in the 3-prism simplex (V = 6, E = 12: 9 cables, 3 struts, D_mech = 12), a generic 0.7 rad twist gives rank(R) = 12 and N_self_stress = 0, whereas the symmetric 30° (π/6 rad) twist drops rank(R) to 11, creating N_self_stress = 1 global prestress across all 12 members that stiffens N_floppy = 1 twist mode at quadratic order. Kane and Lubensky (2014) extended R and Rᵀ to isostatic lattices (N_floppy − N_self_stress = 0), proving topological polarization shifts boundary zero modes across opposite crystal edges.

Maxwell–Calladine Space-Frame Rigidity Index R ∈ ℝ^{E × (3V−6)} , N_floppy − N_self_stress = (3V − 6 − rank(R)) − (E − rank(R)) = (3V − 6) − E

For the V = 8 cube (3V − 6 = 18), 6 face braces give E = 18, rank(R) = 18, N_floppy = 0, N_self_stress = 0; coplanar misplacement at E = 18 gives rank(R) = 17 (1 floppy, 1 self-stress); adding a 7th brace (E = 19) gives rank(R) = 18 (0 floppy, 1 self-stress).

III · Fixing the Tensor Split: The 54 × 15 Normalizer Matrix on su(4)

Now apply the exact same rank-nullity machinery—on its own Lie-algebraic space—to the 2-mass gravitationally induced entanglement witness of Bose et al. (2017) and Marletto and Vedral (2017). At M = 10⁻¹⁴ kg, center separation d = 450 µm, branch split Δx = 250 µm (closest approach d − Δx = 200 µm), and free-fall hold time T = 2.5 s, Newtonian gravity imprints 4 branch phases φ_ij = G M² T / (ℏ d_ij) with net entanglement phase Δφ = φ_LR + φ_RL − φ_LL − φ_RR = 0.313934 rad. On the fixed tensor product ℋ_A ⊗ ℋ_B ≅ ℂ² ⊗ ℂ², the pure 4-branch state has Wootters concurrence |sin(Δφ / 2)| = 0.156323 and Peres–Horodecki partial-transpose negativity 𝒩 = 0.078162. Why can an adversarial skeptic not simply apply a global unitary change of basis U ∈ SU(4) on ℂ⁴ and declare the state unentangled?

Without local observable algebras, that skeptic would succeed completely. Span the 15-dimensional real Lie algebra su(4) of traceless Hermitian 4 × 4 generators by its 15 orthonormal Pauli-pair operators under (1/4) Tr(B_µ B_ν) = δ_µν: 3 local A operators {σ_x ⊗ I, σ_y ⊗ I, σ_z ⊗ I}, 3 local B operators {I ⊗ σ_x, I ⊗ σ_y, I ⊗ σ_z}, and 9 non-local Cartan–Weyl correlation operators {σ_a ⊗ σ_b} for a, b ∈ {x, y, z}. If 0 locality constraints are imposed (E_alg = 0), all 9 non-local directions are unconstrained zero modes. In fact, exponentiating the 15th basis element B₁₅ = σ_z ⊗ σ_z = diag(+1, −1, −1, +1) through the tiny angle θ = Δφ / 4 = 0.078484 rad defines a smooth curve U_nl(θ) = exp(i θ σ_z ⊗ σ_z) ∈ SU(4) that shifts LL and RR by +Δφ/4 and LR and RL by −Δφ/4, cancelling Δφ identically to 0 and collapsing negativity from 𝒩 = 0.078162 to 0! Entanglement on an unpartitioned Hilbert space is as gauge-floppy as an unbraced cube.

Relativistic laboratory physics eliminates those 9 non-local zero modes through spatial microcausality. Trap A and trap B sit in disjoint, spacelike-separated laboratory regions 𝒪_A and 𝒪_B at d − Δx = 200 µm, where Stern–Gerlach readout coils address 2 commuting local observable subalgebras [𝒜_A(x), 𝒜_B(y)] = 0. Any continuous unitary refactorization U(s) = exp(i s X) with X = ∑_{ν=1}^{15} x_ν B_ν that respects the physical laboratory split must map each local subalgebra into itself: −i[X, L_m] ∈ 𝒜_A for m = 1, 2, 3 and −i[X, L_m] ∈ 𝒜_B for m = 4, 5, 6. Because commutators of Pauli tensors with L_m never mix 𝒜_A directly into 𝒜_B, preserving locality is equivalent to requiring zero Hilbert–Schmidt projection onto all 9 non-local basis elements N_q = σ_a ⊗ σ_b across all 6 local generators. That yields an explicit real normalizer-commutator matrix C_loc ∈ ℝ⁵⁴ˣ¹⁵ (6 × 9 = 54 rows, 15 columns). Running exact Gaussian elimination in our kernel proves rank(C_loc) = 9, nullity dim ker(C_loc) = 15 − 9 = 6, and cokernel dim coker(C_loc) = 54 − 9 = 45 (index 6 − 45 = 15 − 54 = −39). All 9 continuous Cartan–Weyl refactorization modes collapse (9 → 0; on B₁₅ alone, ‖C_loc e₁₅‖₂ = 4 > 0), leaving only the 6-dimensional local gauge algebra su(2)_A ⊕ su(2)_B, under whose connected group SU(2)_A × SU(2)_B the BMV negativity 𝒩 = 0.078162 is invariant to machine precision (< 10⁻¹⁴).

Across m = 1..6 local Pauli generators L_m and q = 1..9 non-local Pauli pairs N_q, C_loc annihilates all 6 local generators and locks all 9 non-local Cartan–Weyl directions (9 → 0), fixing 2-mass BMV negativity at 𝒩 = 0.078162 across every connected local unitary U_A ⊗ U_B.

IV · Scope Caveats: Timeless Clock Slices, Discrete SWAP, and Nanoscale Protein Frames

Two sharp mathematical scope boundaries keep our su(4) theorem honest. First, C_loc ∈ ℝ⁵⁴ˣ¹⁵ acts on a fixed conditioned time slice ℋ_A ⊗ ℋ_B (dim su(4) = 15), which must be distinguished from timeless clock-system refactorizations in the conditional-time formalism of Page and Wootters (1983). In Page–Wootters quantum mechanics, a stationary universe state |Ψ⟩ ∈ ℋ_C ⊗ ℋ_S obeys the Wheeler–DeWitt-like constraint (Ĥ_C ⊗ I_S + I_C ⊗ Ĥ_S)|Ψ⟩ = Ĉ|Ψ⟩ = 0, and Schrödinger evolution emerges by conditioning |ψ(τ)⟩_S ∝ ⟨τ|_C Ψ⟩ on clock readings τ. In a 2026 arXiv preprint (PROPOSED epistemic ceiling, unrefereed), Stoica (2026) proves that unitary transformations V̂ inside the commutant [V̂, Ĉ] = 0 preserve Ĉ|Ψ⟩ = 0 while remixing ℋ_C and ℋ_S across slices, altering apparent conditional dynamics and subsystem entanglement whenever Hilbert-space vectors and abstract spectra are all one specifies. What closes that ambiguity in laboratory experiments is operational net locality: once hardware fixes the physical clock observable algebra 𝒜_C and the spacelike-commuting diamond trap algebras [𝒜_A(x), 𝒜_B(y)] = 0 on each conditioned slice, clock-system and inter-trap unitaries outside the local algebra net are physically excluded.

Second, inspect the difference between Lie-algebra tangent modes and disconnected discrete group cosets. Within SU(4), the full group normalizer of su(2)_A ⊕ su(2)_B has 2 connected components, (SU(2)_A × SU(2)_B) ⋊ ℤ₂, where the non-identity coset is generated by the discrete subsystem exchange operator exp(i π/4) SWAP_AB ∈ SU(4) (with det(SWAP_AB) = −1 compensated by exp(i π/4)⁴ = −1 so det = +1). Under conjugation, SWAP_AB exchanges the two local subalgebras 𝒜_A ↔ 𝒜_B wholesale rather than keeping each inside itself. Crucially, because ℤ₂ is discrete, SWAP_AB contributes 0 continuous tangent modes to ker(C_loc) (whose dimension stays 6), is ruled out operationally as soon as trap 𝒪_A and trap 𝒪_B carry distinct spatial coordinates, and—even if applied—leaves bipartite entanglement negativity identically unchanged: 𝒩(SWAP_AB ρ_BMV SWAP_AB†) = 𝒩(ρ_BMV) = 0.078162 to 10⁻¹⁶.

Step down 11 orders of magnitude in length from metre-scale steel trusses to a 10 nm multi-domain membrane protein, and the exact same rank-nullity bar counting reappears in structural biophysics. ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters assemble 2 transmembrane helical bundles and 2 cytosolic nucleotide-binding domains across non-covalent domain interfaces that transmit allosteric motion. In cryo-electron microscopy structures of the human chloride channel CFTR studied in cystic fibrosis research, Fiedorczuk and Chen (2022) resolved how deletion of residue 508 weakens an inter-domain packing junction, leaving cooperative floppy hinge motions (N_floppy > 0) that destabilize the multi-domain frame, whereas 2 small molecules binding distinct interfacial pockets act mechanically as non-coplanar cross-linking struts—adding linearly independent distance constraints across separate domain faces to suppress floppy shear hinges without over-constraining the single functional pore-opening coordinate.
