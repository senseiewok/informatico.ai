Act I — Cool Fact & Human Story: How River Currents Undermine Bridge Piers

A river in flood does its damage where no one can see it. In one survey of 503 United States bridge failures from 1989 to 2000, hydraulic causes such as flood and scour accounted for 266, more than half (Wardhana and Hadipriono 2003, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0887-3828(2003)17:3(144)). Long before a steel girder bends or a concrete deck cracks, the river attacks the hidden bed below. Fast current strikes the rounded nose of a pier, dives toward the bottom, and rolls into a swirling horseshoe vortex that scoops sand and gravel from around the footing. Equilibrium formulas give the hole that weeks of steady flow would dig, and a three-hour mountain flash flood does not last that long. Design practice uses the equilibrium depth (FHWA HEC-18, 2012). A bridge meets many floods, and in clear-water conditions the hole is not refilled between them. Understanding how sediment threshold and flood duration govern that crater separates flash-flood lag from steady-state erosion.

Forensic engineering placed river hydraulics at the center of structural safety when Kumalasari Wardhana and Fabian Hadipriono analyzed 503 bridge failures across the United States from 1989 to 2000 (Journal of Performance of Constructed Facilities, 2003, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0887-3828(2003)17:3(144) [Scite ↗]) and found that 266 failures, or 52.88 percent, stemmed from hydraulic flood, scour, and drift action. Predicting that hazard rests on three historical advances: Albert Shields framed dimensionless bed-shear incipient motion in Berlin in 1936 (recounted by John Buffington in 1999, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(1999)125:4(376) [Scite ↗]); Bruce Melville (1997, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(1997)123:2(125) [Scite ↗]) and Max Sheppard, Melville, and Huseyin Demir (2014, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0000800 [Scite ↗]) unified shallow-flow, flow-intensity, and grain-size multipliers across hundreds of flume and river records; and Melville and Yee-Meng Chiew (1999, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(1999)125:1(59) [Scite ↗]) quantified the multi-day time scale required for clear-water scour pits to reach equilibrium.

Treating pier scour as instantaneous or independent of sediment threshold skews engineering judgment in two opposite directions. In our illustrative reference model for a 2.00 m circular pier in 3.00 m of water over 2.00 mm quartz sand and gravel, mild flow at 0.45 of critical velocity yields 0.608 m of Sheppard and Melville equilibrium scour, whereas the base Colorado State University equation, which has no sediment-threshold term, predicts 1.629 m, or 2.682 times deeper. Conversely, near the clear-water peak at 0.85 of critical velocity, equilibrium scour reaches 2.505 m over 42.09 days, yet a 2.0 hour flash flood carves 1.213 m and a 24.0 hour flood carves 1.817 m. Omitting both grain-size attenuation and flood-duration lag inflates modeled short-flood scour to 4.000 m, more than triple the 2.0 hour model value.

§ I.1 · Obstrucción Clásica y Frontera Estructural

Every enduring equation begins as a human question—an act of patient curiosity by investigators who refused to accept an opaque boundary as the final word. Tracing the intellectual arc of Folio E.16 from its earliest formulation in 1936 (Albert Shields formulates the dimensionless bed-shear threshold for incipient grain motion in Berlin, later documented from archival records by John Buffington (1999, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(1999)125:4(376) [Scite ↗]).) to its modern resolution in 2026 (Abdorreza Kabiri-Samani, Amirhossein Fathi, and Rashid Farooq test clear-water scour in armored beds under steady and unsteady hydrographs (Journal of Hydrology and Hydromechanics, DOI 10.2478/johh-2026-0004 [Scite ↗]).) reveals why un-coupled classical approximations repeatedly stalled at a hard analytical barrier. Whenever investigators attempted to treat the governing relation y_s,SM = 2.5 a* f_1 f_2 f_3 · y_s,CSU = 2.0 K_1 K_2 K_3 y_1 (a* / y_1)^0.65 Fr_1^0.43 · K_t(t_d) = exp(−0.03 |(V_c / V_1) ln((t_d / 24) / t_e)|^1.6) · y_s(t_d) = K_t(t_d) y_s,SM as a purely local or linearized perturbation, higher-order coupling terms across the kernel triad (Shields Threshold (1936) · Melville Pier Scour (1997) · Melville–Chiew Time Scale (1999) · Sheppard–Melville (2014)) amplified truncation error rather than damping it. - Stage 2 (Sheppard–Melville Equilibrium Scour vs. Froude-Only HEC-18 CSU): Sheppard, Melville, and Demir (2014, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0000800) evaluated 23 pier-scour formulas across 569 laboratory and 928 field records (filtered to 441 laboratory and 791 field datasets), merging clear-water factors into y_s,SM = 2.5 a* f_1 f_2 f_3 with f_1 = tanh((y_1 / a*)^0.4), f_2 = max(0, 1 − 1.2 [ln(V_1 / V_c)]^2), and f_3 = (a* / d_50) / [0.4 (a* / d_50)^1.2 + 10.6 (a* / d_50)^(−0.13)]. - Stage 3 (Melville & Chiew Equilibrium Time Scale t_e and Transient Factor K_t): Melville and Chiew (1999, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(1999)125:1(59)) showed that clear-water horseshoe-vortex scour approaches equilibrium over time scale t_e (days) = 30.89 (a* / V_1) (V_1 / V_c − 0.4) (y_1 / a*)^0.25 for y_1 / a* <= 6.0 (where empirical constant 30.89 = 2.669 × 10^6 / 86,400 days·s^(−1) converts dimensionless t* = V_1 t_e,sec / a* with a* in m and V_1 in m/s), with temporal fraction K_t(t_d) = exp(−0.03 |(V_c / V_1) ln((t_d / 24) / t_e)|^1.6) for t_d / 24 < t_e (clamped to 1.0 once t_d / 24 >= t_e). At V_1 / V_c = 0.85 (V_1 = 0.731 m/s), t_e = 42.09 days (1,010.1 h): a 2.0 h flash flood reaches K_t = 0.4841 (y_s = 1.213 m), and a 24.0 h storm reaches K_t = 0.7254 (y_s = 1.817 m). Applying a relation fitted to steady laboratory flows to a 2.00 m pier and to a flood duration is an extrapolation.
- Stage 4 (Open Hypothesis, Tier T1–T2, and Falsification Conditions): Hypothesis (Tier T1–T2): in uniform cohesionless sand or fine gravel around an unskewed circular pier under single-peak clear-water flow (0.45 <= V_1 / V_c <= 1.00), transient scour depth follows y_s(t_d) = K_t(t_d) y_s,SM,eq, excavating roughly half of equilibrium depth within two hours yet remaining below steady-state equilibrium across 24-hour floods (1.817 m vs. 2.505 m S/M and 2.142 m CSU). This hypothesis is falsified under live-bed sediment transport (V_1 / V_c > 1.0), in graded armored riverbeds where horseshoe vortices breach surface armor into finer sub-layers ("armor-break" scour, Kabiri-Samani, Fathi & Farooq 2026, DOI 10.2478/johh-2026-0004), across multi-flood deposition-erosion sequences (Link et al. 2020, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0001696), or when woody debris rafts widen the effective pier obstruction.

def evaluate_pier_scour(
    flow_intensity_ratio: float = 0.85,
    flood_duration_hours: float = 24.0,
    pier_diameter_m: float = 2.00,
    approach_depth_m: float = 3.00,
    median_grain_d50_mm: float = 2.00,
    specific_gravity_s: float = 2.65,
    critical_shields_theta_c: float = 0.045,
    gravity_m_s2: float = 9.81,
    csu_k1: float = 1.0,
    csu_k2: float = 1.0,
    csu_k3: float = 1.1,
    planted_defect: bool = False,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Evaluate Shields threshold, Sheppard-Melville & HEC-18 CSU equilibrium scour, and Melville-Chiew lag."""
    params = (
        flow_intensity_ratio,
        flood_duration_hours,
        pier_diameter_m,
        approach_depth_m,
        median_grain_d50_mm,
        specific_gravity_s,
        critical_shields_theta_c,
        gravity_m_s2,
        csu_k1,
        csu_k2,
        csu_k3,
    )
    if any(isinstance(v, bool) or not math.isfinite(v) for v in params):
        raise ValueError("All hydraulic scour parameters must be finite non-boolean numbers")
    if any(
        v <= 0.0
        for v in (
            flood_duration_hours,
            pier_diameter_m,
            approach_depth_m,
            median_grain_d50_mm,
            critical_shields_theta_c,
            gravity_m_s2,
            csu_k1,
            csu_k2,
            csu_k3,
        )
    ):
        raise ValueError("Geometric, sediment, gravity, and duration parameters must be strictly positive")
    if specific_gravity_s <= 1.0:
        raise ValueError("Sediment specific gravity s = rho_s / rho must exceed 1.0")
    # f_2 crosses zero at exp(-1/sqrt(1.2)) = 0.40137; clear-water regime requires (0.4014, 1.00]
    if not (0.405 <= flow_intensity_ratio <= 1.0):
        raise ValueError("Clear-water flow intensity ratio V_1 / V_c must lie in [0.405, 1.00]")

    d50_m = median_grain_d50_mm * 1.0e-3
    roughness_arg = 5.53 * approach_depth_m / d50_m
    if roughness_arg <= 1.0:
        raise ValueError("Relative depth 5.53 * y_1 / d_50 must exceed 1.0 for logarithmic velocity profile")

    u_star_c = math.sqrt(critical_shields_theta_c * (specific_gravity_s - 1.0) * gravity_m_s2 * d50_m)
    critical_velocity_m_s = 5.75 * u_star_c * math.log10(roughness_arg)
    approach_velocity_m_s = flow_intensity_ratio * critical_velocity_m_s
    froude_1 = approach_velocity_m_s / math.sqrt(gravity_m_s2 * approach_depth_m)

    shallowness_y_over_a = approach_depth_m / pier_diameter_m
    coarseness_a_over_d50 = pier_diameter_m / d50_m

    f1 = math.tanh(shallowness_y_over_a ** 0.4)
    f2 = max(0.0, 1.0 - 1.2 * (math.log(flow_intensity_ratio) ** 2))
    raw_f3 = coarseness_a_over_d50 / (
        0.4 * (coarseness_a_over_d50 ** 1.2) + 10.6 * (coarseness_a_over_d50 ** -0.13)
    )
    f3 = 1.0 if planted_defect else raw_f3

    ys_sm_eq_m = 2.5 * pier_diameter_m * f1 * f2 * f3

    csu_cap = 2.4 if froude_1 <= 0.8 else 3.0
    ys_csu_raw_m = (
        2.0
        * csu_k1
        * csu_k2
        * csu_k3
        * approach_depth_m
        * ((pier_diameter_m / approach_depth_m) ** 0.65)
        * (froude_1 ** 0.43)
    )
    ys_csu_eq_m = min(csu_cap * pier_diameter_m, ys_csu_raw_m)

    # Melville & Chiew (1999) clear-water equilibrium time scale t_e (days), a* in m, V_1 in m/s
    if shallowness_y_over_a <= 6.0:
        te_days = (
            30.89
            * (pier_diameter_m / approach_velocity_m_s)
            * (flow_intensity_ratio - 0.4)
            * (shallowness_y_over_a ** 0.25)
        )
    else:
        te_days = 48.26 * (pier_diameter_m / approach_velocity_m_s) * (flow_intensity_ratio - 0.4)

    flood_days = flood_duration_hours / 24.0
    if planted_defect or flood_days >= te_days:
        kt_factor = 1.0
    else:
        kt_arg = abs((1.0 / flow_intensity_ratio) * math.log(flood_days / te_days))
        kt_factor = math.exp(-0.03 * (kt_arg ** 1.6))

    ys_transient_m = kt_factor * ys_sm_eq_m
    csu_to_transient_ratio = ys_csu_eq_m / ys_transient_m if ys_transient_m > 0.0 else float("inf")

    if kt_factor < 0.65:
        regime = "RAPID INITIAL EXCAVATION (K_t < 0.65, model)"
    elif kt_factor < 0.90:
        regime = "TRANSIENT HYDROGRAPH LAG (0.65 <= K_t < 0.90, model)"
    else:
        regime = "NEAR-EQUILIBRIUM MATURITY (K_t >= 0.90, model)"

    return {
        "folio": "E.16",
        "flow_intensity_ratio": round(flow_intensity_ratio, 3),
        "flood_duration_hours": round(flood_duration_hours, 1),
        "u_star_c_m_s": round(u_star_c, 5),
        "critical_velocity_vc_m_s": round(critical_velocity_m_s, 4),
        "approach_velocity_v1_m_s": round(approach_velocity_m_s, 4),
        "froude_1": round(froude_1, 4),
        "f1_shallowness": round(f1, 4),
        "f2_intensity": round(f2, 4),
        "f3_coarseness": round(f3, 4),
        "equilibrium_time_te_days": round(te_days, 2),
        "equilibrium_time_te_hours": round(te_days * 24.0, 1),
        "time_factor_kt": round(kt_factor, 4),
        "ys_sm_eq_m": round(ys_sm_eq_m, 3),
        "ys_csu_eq_m": round(ys_csu_eq_m, 3),
        "ys_transient_m": round(ys_transient_m, 3),
        "csu_to_transient_ratio": round(csu_to_transient_ratio, 3),
        "regime": regime,
        "exact_ys_sm_eq_m": ys_sm_eq_m,
        "exact_ys_csu_eq_m": ys_csu_eq_m,
        "exact_ys_transient_m": ys_transient_m,
        "exact_kt_factor": kt_factor,
        "exact_te_days": te_days,
    }

    )
    threshold_peak_24h = evaluate_pier_scour(

        flood_duration_hours=24.0,
        planted_defect=planted_defect,

        planted_defect=planted_defect,
    )
    deep_flow_case = evaluate_pier_scour(
        flow_intensity_ratio=0.85,
        flood_duration_hours=24.0,
        pier_diameter_m=0.50,
        approach_depth_m=4.00,  # y_1 / a* = 8.0 > 6.0 exercises the 48.26 deep-flow branch

        flood_duration_hours=24.0,
        pier_diameter_m=0.40,
        approach_depth_m=0.45,
        median_grain_d50_mm=45.0,
        critical_shields_theta_c=0.055,  # Fr_1 > 0.8 exercises the 3.0 * a* CSU cap
        csu_k1=1.1,
        csu_k2=1.4,
        csu_k3=1.1,
        planted_defect=planted_defect,
    )

    # Verify algebraic identity between the two published HEC-18 forms of f_3
    r_val = 2.00 / 0.002
    f3_form_a = r_val / (0.4 * (r_val ** 1.2) + 10.6 * (r_val ** -0.13))
    f3_form_b = (r_val ** 1.13) / (0.4 * (r_val ** 1.33) + 10.6)

    failures: list[str] = []
    if abs(f3_form_a - f3_form_b) > 1e-12:
        failures.append("Sheppard-Melville f_3 algebraic forms diverged")
    expected_deep_te = 48.26 * (0.50 / deep_flow_case["approach_velocity_v1_m_s"]) * (0.85 - 0.4)
    if abs(deep_flow_case["exact_te_days"] - expected_deep_te) > 0.02:
        failures.append(f"Deep-flow y_1/a* > 6.0 branch failed 48.26 time-scale check: {deep_flow_case['exact_te_days']}")
    if high_froude_csu_case["froude_1"] <= 0.8 or abs(high_froude_csu_case["exact_ys_csu_eq_m"] - 3.0 * 0.40) > 1e-9:
        failures.append(
            f"High-Froude CSU 3.0*a* cap check failed: Fr_1={high_froude_csu_case['froude_1']}, y_s,CSU={high_froude_csu_case['ys_csu_eq_m']}"
        )
    if not (flash_2h["exact_ys_transient_m"] < default_24h["exact_ys_transient_m"] < multiday_120h["exact_ys_transient_m"]):
        failures.append("Transient scour depth y_s(t_d) must strictly grow from 2 h to 24 h to 120 h under sub-equilibrium flow")
    if default_24h["exact_kt_factor"] >= 0.90 or flash_2h["exact_kt_factor"] >= 0.60:
        failures.append(
            f"Time-scale lag factor K_t failed flash-flood attenuation bounds (2h={flash_2h['time_factor_kt']}, 24h={default_24h['time_factor_kt']})"
        )
    if default_24h["f3_coarseness"] >= 0.75:
        failures.append(
            f"Grain-coarseness factor f_3={default_24h['f3_coarseness']} failed to attenuate sand-scale pier ratio a*/d_50=1000"
        )
    if not (default_24h["exact_ys_transient_m"] < default_24h["exact_ys_csu_eq_m"] < default_24h["exact_ys_sm_eq_m"]):
        failures.append("Expected 24h ordering y_s(24h) < y_s,CSU,eq < y_s,SM,eq violated at V_1/V_c=0.85")
    if not (post_eq_low_intensity["exact_ys_sm_eq_m"] < post_eq_low_intensity["exact_ys_csu_eq_m"]):
        failures.append("Near-threshold V_1/V_c=0.45 must keep threshold-aware y_s,SM,eq below Froude-only y_s,CSU,eq")
    if threshold_peak_24h["exact_ys_sm_eq_m"] <= default_24h["exact_ys_sm_eq_m"]:
        failures.append("Equilibrium clear-water scour y_s,SM must peak at incipient live-bed threshold V_1/V_c = 1.00")

            ("K_t at 2 h", flash_2h["exact_kt_factor"], 0.4841, 5e-5),
            ("y_s at 2 h", flash_2h["exact_ys_transient_m"], 1.213, 5e-4),
            ("K_t at 24 h", default_24h["exact_kt_factor"], 0.7254, 5e-5),
            ("y_s at 24 h", default_24h["exact_ys_transient_m"], 1.817, 5e-4),
            ("K_t at 120 h", multiday_120h["exact_kt_factor"], 0.8777, 5e-5),
            ("y_s at 120 h", multiday_120h["exact_ys_transient_m"], 2.199, 5e-4),
            ("y_s,SM at 0.45", low_intensity_24h["exact_ys_sm_eq_m"], 0.608, 5e-4),

        "status": status,
        "folio": "E.16",
