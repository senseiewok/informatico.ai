In the early nineteen-seventies, two pioneering computer scientists named Stephen Cook and Richard Karp drew a monumental boundary across the landscape of computation, dividing problems into tractable polynomial tasks and intractable exponential labyrinths. Yet as the digital age advanced into the twenty-first century, that classical dividing line began to fray.
