I · Five-sixths of gravitating matter is unseen in light, and gravity is universal

Across cosmological and cluster scales, most gravitating mass neither emits nor scatters light: the Planck 2018 cosmic microwave background power spectra measure a physical cold dark matter density Ω_c h² = 0.1200 ± 0.0012 beside baryon density Ω_b h² = 0.02237 ± 0.00015—a dark-to-baryonic ratio of 5.364 ± 0.065, or 84.29% of non-relativistic matter—and weak gravitational lensing of the merging Bullet Cluster 1E 0657-558 shows the gravitational potential peaks separated at 8σ from the X-ray-emitting plasma that holds most of the normal baryonic gas. Meanwhile, laboratory and space tests confirm that gravitational proper-time redshift Δf/f = ΔΦ/c² is measured down to a 33 cm height difference in optical aluminium-ion clocks and that the Weak Equivalence Principle holds to η = (−1.5 ± 2.7)×10⁻¹⁵ in MICROSCOPE.

In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation

Imagine an invisible bowling ball rolling across a stretched rubber sheet in a pitch-dark room. Flashlights shine right through the ball without casting a shadow or bouncing a single gleam back to your eyes—in the language of T.11, the ball is unrendered in the photon bath. Yet the sheet still dips wherever the ball rolls.

If you place two precision clocks at different spots on the sheet, the clock closer to the passing ball sits slightly deeper in the gravitational dip and ticks a fraction slower while the ball goes by. Even if two sectors of matter never exchange a photon, they still share the same spacetime geometry and meet through the rate of time.

Everyday Analogy & Controls Primer

Everyday analogy: two tide gauges anchored a kilometre apart in a harbour at night. A submerged submarine with all lights off reflects no radar and shines no lamp above the water, yet its hull displaces water as it passes near the first pier: comparing the two gauges reveals the transit from the difference in water level alone, whereas two gauges bolted to the exact same post rise and fall together and see zero difference.

How the controls work in plain words: the first slider changes the spatial baseline d_c between Clock 1 and Clock 2; setting d_c = 0 puts both clocks at the same point, where the pure-gravity proper-time difference vanishes. The second slider sets a hypothetical non-gravitational scalar coupling d_e to the electromagnetic fine-structure constant, which optical clock pairs test even at zero baseline. The knockout sets G_dark = 0: the gravitational dip vanishes and the verifier rejects the run.
