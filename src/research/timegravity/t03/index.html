What Sets the Slope

On the previous page the slope in the rate of time was a number put in by hand. Here it has a source: mass-energy. In the weak field the Einstein equation reduces to Poisson's equation, the instrument solves it on a grid, and every body, whatever it is made of, reads the same map. That sameness has been measured to a part in a thousand trillion, and antimatter has been seen to fall once.

I · Every composition falls alike, to a part in 10¹⁵

Bodies of different composition fall with the same acceleration: the MICROSCOPE satellite found η(Ti, Pt) = (−1.5 ± 2.3 statistical ± 1.5 systematic) × 10⁻¹⁵ for titanium against platinum in 2022; the Eöt-Wash rotating torsion balance found η(Be, Ti) = (0.3 ± 1.8) × 10⁻¹³ in 2008; and lunar laser ranging finds the Earth and the Moon, which differ in composition and in gravitational self-energy, falling toward the Sun alike at the 10⁻¹³ level. Three collaborations, three methods, in orbit, on a bench and across 384,000 km: that is why the claim may say MEASURED.

Antimatter falls down: in 2023 the ALPHA collaboration released antihydrogen atoms in a vertical trap at CERN and measured a downward acceleration of (0.75 ± 0.13 statistical and systematic ± 0.16 simulation) g. One collaboration, one apparatus, a result consistent with g at about one sigma: the row is SINGLE RESULT, and a reported internal repeat in 2024 does not change that, because an internal repeat is not an independent replication.

In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation

The last page said: where time runs slower below than above, things drift downward. This page asks what makes time run slower below. The answer is: mass. Put a planet somewhere and the clocks around it slow down, more the closer they are. The instrument computes that slowing from the planet's mass, with the same equation Newton would have used, because in weak gravity Einstein's equation becomes Newton's.

And because what falls is the clock rate, not the object, a feather, a hammer and an atom of antihydrogen all read the same map. Experiments have checked that to fifteen decimal places. Nobody has found a material that reads a different map.

Everyday Analogy & Controls Primer

Everyday analogy: a heavy sleeper lies down on a trampoline and the fabric dips around them; the dip is deepest right beside them and fades with distance. The dip is the clock-rate map, the sleeper is the mass, and the equation that gives the shape of the dip from the weight is Poisson's. Roll a marble and a bowling ball across the fabric from the same spot and they curve toward the sleeper along the same path, because the fabric does not ask what they are made of.

How the controls work in plain words: the mass slider is how heavy the sleeper is; the rings redraw and the surface acceleration readout changes. The knockout lifts the sleeper off the trampoline: flat fabric, nothing rolls. The planted defect is a trampoline that treats the marble and the bowling ball differently, which no experiment has ever seen; the readout shows the separation such a fabric would produce after one second.

II · From the field equation to Poisson, and why composition cannot enter

g₀₀ ≈ −(1 + 2Φ/c²), G₀₀ = (8πG/c⁴) T₀₀, T₀₀ ≈ ρc² ⇒ ∇²Φ = 4πGρ

In the weak-field, slow-motion, static limit of the 1916 field equation, the time-time component of the metric is g₀₀ ≈ −(1 + 2Φ/c²) and the 00 component of the field equation becomes Poisson's equation ∇²Φ = 4πGρ; the slope A′(z) = 2g/c² that the previous page put in by hand is 2∇Φ/c², and its source is the mass-energy density on the right-hand side. The parent MEASURED row is the redshift measurement that fixed the g₀₀ dependence on the potential; the derivation row is Einstein's 1916 paper. The instrument solves the equation on a 2,000-point radial grid for a uniform sphere and checks the result against −GM/r outside the body.

The geodesic equation contains no property of the falling body, no mass, charge or composition, so once the map Φ is fixed every body reads it alike; a composition-dependent fall, η ≠ 0, would mean the geodesic picture is wrong, which is what the equivalence-principle experiments test and what the verifier's planted defect imitates. This is the page's knockout turned inside out: the previous page removed the gradient and nothing fell; this one removes the source and the gradient goes with it.

Stage 1 · The limit

Weak field (|Φ| ≪ c²), slow motion (v ≪ c), static source. Then g₀₀ ≈ −(1 + 2Φ/c²), T₀₀ ≈ ρc², and G₀₀ ≈ (2/c²)∇²Φ, so the 00 equation reads ∇²Φ = 4πGρ.

Stage 2 · The grid

Spherical symmetry: (1/r²) d/dr (r² dΦ/dr) = 4πGρ(r), discretised in conservative form on 2,000 cells out to 4R, with dΦ/dr = 0 at the centre and Φ = −GM/r_max at the edge, solved by the Thomas algorithm. The surface cell takes the volume-weighted density so no half-cell of mass is invented.

Stage 3 · The checks

Outside the body Φ must equal −GM/r within 2e-3 everywhere and the surface acceleration must equal GM/R² within 2e-3; at the centre Φ(0) must equal −3GM/2R. Two bodies released together with η = 0 must separate by less than 1e-12 m after one second.

Stage 4 · The defect

The planted defect sets the Eötvös parameter η = 2(a₁ − a₂)/(a₁ + a₂) to 10⁻³. The bodies then separate by about 4.9 mm in one second and the equivalence check fails, as it must, or the page does not build.

III · What this page does not say

What fixes the value of G, and whether the equivalence principle holds exactly or only to the 10⁻¹⁵ reached so far, are not questions the measurements answer; the field's standard survey lists them among the open tests, and every proposed violation remains a proposal. A 2026 result on the equivalence principle for quantum phases is not in the ledger because no DOI for it resolved when this page was built; the ledger admits rows, not headlines.

The page also does not say what happens when the source itself is in a quantum superposition, in two places at once. Poisson's equation takes a density; it has nothing to say about a mass that has no single position. That is the next page's question.

def verify_reference_files() -> dict:
    manifest = json.loads((FILES_DIR / "manifest.json").read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
    out = {}
    for entry in manifest["files"]:
        raw = (FILES_DIR / entry["filename"]).read_bytes()
        assert len(raw) == entry["bytes"], f"{entry['filename']}: byte count mismatch"
        digest = hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()
        assert digest == entry["sha256"], f"{entry['filename']}: sha256 mismatch"
        out[entry["filename"]] = digest[:12]
    ref = json.loads((FILES_DIR / "t03_reference.json").read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
