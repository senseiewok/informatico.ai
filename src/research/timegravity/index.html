Time & Gravity

A common intuition is that experience slides along a worldline and that gravity is a slope in the rate of time. Is any of that physics? Half of it is, and it is among the best-measured facts we have. The other half has no variable in any equation. These nine pages walk from the measured half to the open half without inventing a step.

Fixed boundary · printed on every T page · asserted by the lint Nothing on this page is a theory of everything, and this site proposes none. Every claim carries one of six labels, each tied to a ledger row: MEASURED, DERIVED, BOUND, PROPOSED, OPEN, or SINGLE RESULT. Preprints never rise above PROPOSED. Each page ends with one open question that the next page takes up.

The question, drawn once

A picture of the question, not a result. The horizontal axis is coordinate time, the vertical axis is height, and the shading is how fast a clock runs at each height, darker meaning slower. The marker is 'now' as the intuition imagines it, a point sliding along the worldline; move it with the slider or the arrow keys. General relativity has the worldline and the shading. It has no marker. Which pages carry that difference is what the map below is for.

The slider moves a picture, not a variable. No equation on the pages that follow has a term for it.

How to read a T page

Every page in this track ships six things, and the build refuses a page that lacks one: a hand-written instrument whose knockout is the argument; a standard-library verifier that recomputes the page's central number and fails on a planted defect; a grounding ledger whose every DOI resolves in the site's offline Crossref cache; a label on every claim, derived from the ledger by a lint and decided by a person; a SKILL.md so any agent can rebuild the toy; and a Mexican Spanish twin in the same commit.

  • At least two collaborations, peer-reviewed DOIs, older than the replication window.
  • Follows by mathematics from a named MEASURED parent row.
  • An exclusion limit: what the data rule out, not what they show.
  • One collaboration, or anything inside the last twenty-four months.
  • A proposal or a preprint; a preprint can never be more.
  • A question the field has not closed.

The labels describe evidence, not confidence. MEASURED needs at least two collaborations with peer-reviewed results. BOUND is an exclusion limit. DERIVED follows by mathematics from a named MEASURED parent. SINGLE RESULT is one collaboration, or anything published inside the last twenty-four months. PROPOSED is a proposal, and a preprint can never be more. OPEN is a question the field has not closed. A lint checks the mechanics: rows, dates, collaborations, DOIs, the same label sequence in both languages. A human checklist decides the label itself.

This hub carries no claims at all, and the lint asserts that. It states the intuition once, above, as a question in the third person, and that sentence is banned on every other page of the track.
