The Equation with No Time

Canonical quantum gravity hands physics an equation with no time in it: Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0. Page and Wootters showed how a clock inside the state can still read time: condition the stationary whole on the clock, and the rest evolves exactly as Schrödinger says. This page builds that toy on two small subsystems, checks it against the closed form, and shows that a different choice of clock gives a different time, or none.

I · What a two-level system does, measured since 1938

A two-level quantum system evolves in time as Schrödinger's equation says: the molecular-beam resonance of 1938 drove it at its Bohr frequency, the separated oscillatory fields of 1950 read the phase it accumulated between two interaction zones, and the spin echoes of the same year refocused its free precession with a single pulse. Three collaborations, three methods, one equation of motion. Everything on this page that is DERIVED derives from this; the toy below must reproduce exactly that evolution or it is wrong.

In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation

Imagine a photograph of a whole room, with a wall clock in it and a pendulum swinging. The photograph does not change. Yet if you look at the part of the photograph where the clock says one o'clock, the pendulum is here; where the clock says two, the pendulum is there. The photograph has no time in it, and still, relative to the clock, the pendulum moves. That is the whole idea of this page.

Now imagine you decided to use the pendulum as the clock and the wall clock as the thing being watched. The story is different. And if you chose to read the wall clock by its color instead of its hands, nothing in the photograph would move at all. Which part is the clock is a choice, and that choice is where the open question lives.

Everyday Analogy & Controls Primer

Everyday analogy: a long film strip laid flat on a table. Every frame is already there; the strip does not move. 'Time' is the frame number printed in the corner. Pick a frame number and you see where the actors stand; walk along the strip and they act out the story. Sort the frames by brightness instead of by number and the story is gone, though not one frame changed.

How the controls work in plain words: the slider is the frame number, the clock reading. The knockout sorts the frames by a different label, the clock's energy, and the right-hand plot goes flat: no story. The planted defect prints the frame numbers in the wrong order, so the strip no longer matches the rule that generated it; the residual readout catches that.

The Humans Behind Step T.07 · A Timeless Equation, Conditional Clocks, and Two Entangled Photons

When canonical quantum gravity removed external coordinate time from the wave equation of a closed universe, these physicists asked how a ticking clock can live inside the quantum state itself.

1967 · Chapel Hill & Princeton

Bryce S. DeWitt & John Archibald Wheeler

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill & Princeton University

In his 1967 treatise on canonical quantum gravity, following discussions with John Archibald Wheeler, Bryce DeWitt showed that reparameterization invariance forces the total Hamiltonian of a closed universe to annihilate the state: Ĥ|Ψ⟩ = 0, leaving no external time parameter t.

Wheeler–DeWitt constraint · Ĥ|Ψ⟩ = 0 (DERIVED) DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.160.1113 ↗

1983 · University Park & Williamstown

Don N. Page & William K. Wootters

Pennsylvania State University & Williams College

Page and Wootters proposed 'evolution without evolution': split the stationary world into a clock subsystem C and the rest S with (Ĥ_C + Ĥ_S)|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0. Projecting the stationary joint state onto the clock's time states |t_n⟩_C recovers the exact Schrödinger evolution of S.

Conditional probability clock · |ψ(t_n)⟩ = √d ⟨t_n|Ψ⟩⟩ DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885 ↗

2014 & 2019 · Turin, Pisa, Pavia & Vienna

Ekaterina Moreva, Giorgio Brida, Marco Gramegna, Vittorio Giovannetti, Lorenzo Maccone & Marco Genovese · Flaminia Giacomini, Esteban Castro-Ruiz & Časlav Brukner

INRIM National Metrology Institute (Turin) & IQOQI Vienna

In 2014 Moreva and colleagues built a two-photon optical-table realization in Turin: passing polarization-entangled photons through quartz plates, they showed that conditioning on one photon's polarization recovers the other's rotation while an unconditioned observer sees a stationary state. In 2019 Giacomini, Castro-Ruiz, and Brukner derived the transformations between quantum reference frames.

II · From the constraint to a clock that reads time

Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0, Ĥ = Ĥ_C ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ Ĥ_S, |Ψ⟩⟩ = (1/√d) Σₙ |tₙ⟩ ⊗ e^{−iĤ_S tₙ}|ψ(0)⟩

In the canonical formulation of general relativity the Hamiltonian is a constraint: physical states satisfy Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0, so the state of the whole does not evolve and no external time parameter appears in the equation. The toy on this page is not gravity; it keeps only that structure: a constraint, a whole with no outside, and two parts.

Page and Wootters showed that if the whole contains a clock subsystem, conditioning the stationary state on the clock reading tₙ leaves the rest in e^{−iĤ_S tₙ}|ψ(0)⟩: ordinary Schrödinger evolution, recovered from a state that never changes; the verifier builds this on a 16-level cyclic clock and a qubit and finds P(+x | tₙ) = cos²(ε tₙ/2) to one part in 10¹⁰ while ‖Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩‖ stays at 10⁻¹⁵. The check is against the closed form, not against a simulation of itself: the cos² is the two-level evolution the first claim on this page calls MEASURED.

Which subsystem is the clock, and in which basis it is read, is a choice of reference frame: read the same state in the clock's energy basis and every conditional state is an energy eigenstate with P(+x) = 1/2, no evolution at all; quantum reference frames make this frame dependence explicit, with physical laws covariant under the change of frame. That is the knockout: a different split gives a different time, and this split gives none.

In 2014 Moreva and colleagues illustrated the construction with photons: the polarisation of one photon served as the clock, and the second photon's conditional state showed evolution while the pair as a whole was stationary. It remains the only realisation, so the row is SINGLE RESULT; it is an illustration of the mathematics, not a test of quantum gravity.

Stage 1 · The constraint

Ĥ = Ĥ_C ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ Ĥ_S on a 16-level clock and a qubit, 32 dimensions in all. Clock energies Eₘ = 2πm/d, m = −8…7; qubit Ĥ_S = (ε/2)σ_z with ε/2 = 2π/d, so both qubit energies sit on the clock lattice and a zero-energy state can exist.

Stage 2 · The history state

|Ψ⟩⟩ = (1/√d) Σₙ |tₙ⟩ ⊗ e^{−iĤ_S tₙ}|+x⟩ with Pegg–Barnett time states |tₙ⟩ = (1/√d) Σₘ e^{−iEₘtₙ}|Eₘ⟩. Each clock-energy component pairs with the qubit energy that cancels it, so Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0 exactly; the verifier reports the residual.

Stage 3 · Conditioning and the other split

Project onto |tₙ⟩ and normalise: the qubit is in e^{−iĤ_S tₙ}|+x⟩ and P(+x | tₙ) = cos²(ε tₙ/2), the closed form. Project onto |Eₘ⟩ instead and the qubit is an energy eigenstate: P(+x) = 1/2 for every m, no evolution.

Stage 4 · Eigensolver and defect

Ĥ is written in the time ⊗ σ_x basis, a dense 32×32 Hermitian matrix, embedded as a real symmetric 64×64 matrix and diagonalised with a cyclic Jacobi sweep in pure Python. The zero-eigenvalue subspace has dimension 2 and holds the state with projection 1. The planted defect bends the clock spectrum to m·|m|; the residual rises to about 13 and the check fails, as it must.

III · What this page does not say

A 2026 preprint argues that the conditional construction still carries a preferred-time problem: the choice of clock and basis that makes the recovered evolution look like ordinary physics is not fixed by the constraint itself. A preprint cannot rise above PROPOSED here, whatever its merits; the ledger row carries a review date for when peer review lands.

Whose clock the universe keeps, if any subsystem's, is not settled: the construction recovers a time for every choice of clock, and nothing in the constraint picks one. The 'felt now' that the hub stated as a question is absent here too: the toy has readings, not a present. That absence is the page's honest result, and the next page asks what happens when acceleration or gravity turns the split between clock and rest into a causal horizon.

## 4. Deterministic verification and falsification rules
1. `‖Ĥ|Ψ⟩⟩‖ < 1e-10`: the global state is stationary.
2. `max_n |P(+x | tₙ) − cos²(ε tₙ/2)| < 1e-10`: conditioning on the clock recovers Schrödinger evolution, asserted against the closed form, never against seeded random numbers.
3. Conditioning on the clock's energy basis gives `P(+x) = 1/2` for every reading with non-zero weight: a different split, no time.
4. The Jacobi eigensolver on the real embedding finds a zero-eigenvalue subspace of dimension ≥ 1 that contains the state (projection 1 within 1e-8).
5. Planted defect: `Eₘ ∝ m·|m|` makes the clock's generator disagree with the constraint; rule 1 fails and `--planted-defect` exits non-zero.

def verify_reference_files() -> dict:
    manifest = json.loads((FILES_DIR / "manifest.json").read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
    out = {}
    for entry in manifest["files"]:
        raw = (FILES_DIR / entry["filename"]).read_bytes()
        assert len(raw) == entry["bytes"], f"{entry['filename']}: byte count mismatch"
        digest = hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()
        assert digest == entry["sha256"], f"{entry['filename']}: sha256 mismatch"
        out[entry["filename"]] = digest[:12]
    ref = json.loads((FILES_DIR / "t07_reference.json").read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
    assert ref["constraint_residual"] < PW_TOL, "reference history state is not stationary"
    assert ref["jacobi_kernel"]["kernel_dim"] >= 1, "reference kernel is empty"
