Act I — Cool Fact & Human Story: Why Damp Sand Castles Stand and Steep Hills Slip Before Flooding

Anyone who has built a sand castle at low tide has watched an apparent geotechnical paradox up close: dry sand pours out into a flat cone near 32°, completely submerged sand slumps flat under water, yet damp sand stands in vertical walls. The hidden clamp holding those grains together is capillary water under tension—negative gauge pore pressure above the water table, called matric suction ψ = u_a − u_w. In 1976, Yechezkel Mualem linked pore-size distribution to unsaturated hydraulic conductivity (Water Resources Research, DOI 10.1029/WR012i003p00513), and in 1980 Martinus van Genuchten turned that integral into a smooth closed-form Soil–Water Characteristic Curve (Soil Science Society of America Journal, DOI 10.2136/sssaj1980.03615995004400050002x). Meanwhile, Fredlund, Morgenstern, and Widger (1978, Canadian Geotechnical Journal, DOI 10.1139/t78-029) formalized shear strength in unsaturated soils using net normal stress and matric suction as two independent stress-state variables. Today, understanding when capillary suction holds a hillside above its friction angle—and how rapidly storm rainfall erases that suction—sits at the center of rainfall-induced landslide forecasting.

In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation

In plain words, soil above the groundwater table is neither bone-dry nor completely flooded. Tiny curved water bridges cling between soil grains and pull them inward, acting like millions of microscopic rubber bands. As long as the soil stays damp but not saturated, those capillary bridges supply extra clamping force—called suction stress—that can hold a hillside steeper than loose sand or silt could otherwise stand. During heavy rain, water soaking down from the surface fills the air spaces, relaxes those curved menisci, and drops the clamping force even before free groundwater pressure rises above zero. If the slope is steeper than its drained friction angle, losing that suction can trigger a sudden shallow slide.

In 2000, Richard M. Iverson coupled transient infiltration physics with infinite-slope stability to show how storm intensity, hydraulic diffusivity, and antecedent moisture control the timing of shallow landslides (Water Resources Research, DOI DOI 10.1029/2000WR900090 [Scite ↗]). Eight years later, Ning Lu and Jonathan W. Godt (2008, DOI DOI 10.1029/2008WR006976 [Scite ↗]) and Lu, Godt, and Wu (2010, DOI DOI 10.1029/2009WR008646 [Scite ↗]) bridged Bishop's empirical effective-stress weighting χ with van Genuchten's retention parameters via a single closed-form suction stress: σ^s(ψ) = −S_e(ψ) ψ. In a silty-loam colluvium (α_vG = 0.02 kPa^−1, n = 1.60, γ = 19.0 kN/m^3, c' = 2.0 kPa, φ' = 28.0°, slip-plane depth z = 2.0 m) on a 34.0° slope, a moderate matric suction of ψ = 24.0 kPa maintains effective saturation S_e = 0.904, suction stress σ^s = −21.69 kPa, and an infinite-slope Factor of Safety FS = 1.557. Wetting the soil down to ψ = 2.0 kPa—still under negative pore-water pressure—drops σ^s to −2.00 kPa and sends FS down to 0.962, below limit equilibrium.

Distinguishing true effective suction stress −S_e ψ from naive linear Terzaghi extrapolation −ψ matters directly for hazard screening on roads, levees, and mountain catchments. In coarse clean sand (α_vG = 0.145 kPa^−1, n = 2.68, γ = 18.0 kN/m^3, c' = 0 kPa, φ' = 32.0°, β = 36.0°), capillary menisci break apart once suction exceeds the air-entry peak at ψ_peak = 7.964 kPa (σ^s = −4.52 kPa, FS = 1.025). By ψ = 40.0 kPa, effective saturation drops to S_e = 0.052, suction stress shrinks to −2.08 kPa, and real FS falls to 0.936 (< 1.0). Multiplying full ψ by χ = 1.0 instead of S_e would predict −40.0 kPa of fictitious clamping and FS = 2.320, masking why dry or overly coarse slopes fail above their friction angle.

How the Two Sliders Work in Plain Words: Our interactive geotechnical laboratory exposes two primary parameters from the closed-form screening model. The first slider is Matric Suction at the Slip Plane (ψ = u_a − u_w, from 0.0 to 120.0 kPa), representing capillary tension at depth z = 2.0 m inside the silty-loam slope. Dragging ψ toward 0.0 kPa simulates storm wetting: relative permeability K_r surges from 0.1662 toward 1.0000 while suction stress |σ^s| collapses toward 0.00 kPa and FS crosses below 1.0 near ψ = 3.27 kPa. The second slider is Slope Inclination (β, from 20.0° to 45.0°), showing how slopes gentler than φ' = 28.0° remain above limit equilibrium even saturated, whereas steeper slopes rely on suction.

In van Genuchten (1980) with Mualem constraint m = 1 − 1/n (n > 1), effective saturation S_e(ψ) = [1 + (α_vG ψ)^n]^(−m) drops smoothly from 1 at ψ = 0 toward 0 at high suction. Multiplying S_e by ψ yields the Lu–Godt–Wu suction stress σ^s(ψ) = −S_e(ψ) ψ ≤ 0, so effective normal stress on a plane at depth z becomes σ'_n = γ z cos²β − σ^s = γ z cos²β + S_e ψ. Differentiating |σ^s(ψ)| yields a critical exponent split at n = 2: for 1 < n ≤ 2 (silty loam, n = 1.60), d|σ^s|/dψ remains positive for all ψ ≥ 0; for n > 2 (clean sand, n = 2.68), d|σ^s|/dψ flips negative past ψ_peak = [α_vG (n − 2)^(1/n)]^(−1) = 7.964 kPa. Notice the modeling boundaries: α_vG is in kPa^−1, γ is held constant rather than varying as γ(θ), and ψ is prescribed uniformly at z = 2.0 m. Live parameter coupling: Matric Suction at Slip Plane = 24.0 kPa under governing law .

# Unsaturated Soil Suction Stress, van Genuchten–Mualem Water Retention & Rainfall-Induced Infinite-Slope Instability (Folio E.14)

A plain-text companion to the essay at https://informatico.ai/engineering/e14/ (`E.14 · Unsaturated Suction Stress & Slope Margin · REV 1.0`, `REV 1.0`, redated `2026-11-21`).

## The governing relation
`S_e = [1 + (α_vG ψ)^n]^(−m) · σ^s = −S_e ψ · FS = tan φ' / tan β + (c' − σ^s tan φ') / (γ z sin β cos β)`

## Rules the essay derives
1. Enforce the physical domain psi = u_a - u_w >= 0 kPa, alpha_vg > 0 kPa^-1, and n > 1.0 (m = 1 - 1/n in (0, 1)) for van Genuchten effective saturation S_e(psi) = [1 + (alpha_vg * psi)^n]^(-m) and Mualem relative hydraulic conductivity K_r(S_e).
2. Compute Lu-Godt-Wu closed-form suction stress as sigma_s(psi) = -S_e(psi) * psi <= 0 (with sigma_s = 0 at psi = 0), keeping effective weighting chi = S_e rather than extrapolating Terzaghi chi = 1.0 into unsaturated soil.
3. Distinguish fine-grained monotonic retention (1 < n <= 2, silty loam where |sigma_s| grows monotonically with suction) from coarse-grained non-monotonic retention (n > 2, clean sand where |sigma_s| peaks near air-entry and declines toward zero upon drying).
4. Frame infinite-slope stability as a steady-suction, constant-gamma Tier T1-T2 screening hypothesis that requires 3D seepage, wetting-drying hysteresis, and field tensiometry before any site-level slope evaluation.

## How it works, in four stages
- Stage 1 (van Genuchten–Mualem Water Retention, Continuing E.05): Above the water table (ψ = u_a − u_w ≥ 0 kPa), soil pores hold both air and capillary water. van Genuchten (1980, DOI 10.2136/sssaj1980.03615995004400050002x) parameterizes effective saturation as S_e(ψ) = [1 + (α_vG ψ)^n]^(−m) with m = 1 − 1/n (requiring n > 1), and Mualem (1976, DOI 10.1029/WR012i003p00513) gives K_r(S_e) = S_e^(1/2) [1 − (1 − S_e^(1/m))^m]^2. In silty loam (α_vG = 0.02 kPa^−1, n = 1.60, m = 0.375) at ψ = 24.0 kPa, S_e = 0.904 and K_r = 0.1662.
- Stage 2 (Lu–Godt–Wu Suction Stress σ^s and Pore-Exponent Split): Lu, Godt & Wu (2010, DOI 10.1029/2009WR008646) unify Bishop effective stress σ' = (σ − u_a) − σ^s via closed-form suction stress σ^s(ψ) = −S_e(ψ) ψ ≤ 0 (compression-positive convention, so negative σ^s pulls grains together). For silty loam (1 < n ≤ 2), |σ^s| rises monotonically (−21.69 kPa at ψ = 24.0 kPa; −65.34 kPa at ψ = 120.0 kPa). For clean sand (n = 2.68 > 2, α_vG = 0.145 kPa^−1), |σ^s| peaks at ψ_peak = 7.964 kPa (−4.52 kPa) and declines to −2.08 kPa by ψ = 40.0 kPa.
- Stage 3 (Infinite-Slope Wetting Collapse While Still Unsaturated): Following Lu & Godt (2008, DOI 10.1029/2008WR006976) with steady uniform suction at z = 2.0 m, γ = 19.0 kN/m^3, c' = 2.0 kPa, and φ' = 28.0°, a steep silty-loam slope at β = 34.0° (> φ') stands at FS = 1.557 when ψ = 24.0 kPa. Rain wetting to ψ = 2.0 kPa drops FS to 0.962 (< 1.0) even before pore-water pressure turns positive, and saturation (ψ = 0 kPa, K_r = 1.0) drops FS to 0.902.
- Stage 4 (Open Hypothesis, Tier T1–T2, and Falsification Condition): Hypothesis (Tier T1–T2): in shallow unsaturated hillslopes steeper than their drained friction angle (β > φ'), storm-triggered collapse below FS = 1.0 tracks the loss of S_e ψ during wetting rather than waiting for positive u_w, whereas setting Bishop χ = 1.0 on dry clean sand (ψ = 40.0 kPa, γ = 18.0 kN/m^3, c' = 0, φ' = 32°, β = 36°) falsely inflates FS from 0.936 to 2.320. This 1D steady-suction hypothesis is falsified where 3D topographic flow convergence, macropore bypass (Iverson 2000, DOI 10.1029/2000WR900090), wetting-branch hysteresis, or root cohesion governs field failure.

"""Deterministic verifier for Folio E.14: Unsaturated Soil Suction Stress, van Genuchten-Mualem Retention & Infinite-Slope Stability.

Sign convention (compression-positive geomechanics, matching E.05 and E.13):
  - Matric suction (gauge pore-air pressure u_a = 0): psi = u_a - u_w >= 0 (kPa).
  - Effective saturation (van Genuchten 1980, DOI 10.2136/sssaj1980.03615995004400050002x):
        S_e(psi) = [1 + (alpha_vg * psi)^n]^(-m),   m = 1 - 1/n,   n > 1.
  - Relative hydraulic conductivity (Mualem 1976, DOI 10.1029/WR012i003p00513):
        K_r(S_e) = S_e^(1/2) * [1 - (1 - S_e^(1/m))^m]^2.
  - Closed-form suction stress (Lu, Godt & Wu 2010, DOI 10.1029/2009WR008646):
        sigma_s(psi) = -S_e(psi) * psi <= 0 (tensile interparticle capillary/adsorptive attraction).
    Effective normal stress on slip plane: sigma'_n = (sigma_n - u_a) - sigma_s = (sigma_n - u_a) + S_e * psi.
  - Infinite-slope Factor of Safety at slip-plane depth z (Lu & Godt 2008, DOI 10.1029/2008WR006976):
        FS = tan(phi') / tan(beta) + (c' - sigma_s * tan(phi')) / (gamma * z * sin(beta) * cos(beta)).

Model scope & caveats:
  - Prescribes steady uniform matric suction psi >= 0 at depth z rather than solving coupled transient
    Richards infiltration psi(z, t) or moisture-dependent unit weight gamma(theta).
  - alpha_vg is in kPa^-1 (1 kPa ~ 10.194 cm H2O).
  - Below the water table (u_w > u_a, psi < 0), this unsaturated domain transitions to saturated Terzaghi
    effective stress (S_e = 1, K_r = 1, sigma_s = -psi = u_w - u_a > 0).

Planted-defect mode (`--planted-defect`):
  Sets Bishop weighting chi = 1.0 (extrapolating saturated Terzaghi linearly as sigma_s = -psi instead of
  -S_e * psi) across unsaturated soil, falsely predicting slope stability (FS > 1) in clean sand at
  psi = 40.0 kPa where actual desaturation leaves S_e = 0.052 and FS < 1.0.
"""
from __future__ import annotations

import argparse
import json
import math
import sys
from typing import Any


def evaluate_unsaturated_slope(
    matric_suction_psi_kpa: float = 24.0,
    slope_beta_deg: float = 34.0,
    alpha_vg_inv_kpa: float = 0.02,
    van_genuchten_n: float = 1.60,
    unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3: float = 19.0,
    cohesion_c_eff_kpa: float = 2.0,
    friction_phi_eff_deg: float = 28.0,
    slip_depth_z_m: float = 2.0,
    planted_defect: bool = False,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Compute van Genuchten S_e, Mualem K_r, Lu-Godt-Wu suction stress sigma_s, and infinite-slope FS."""
    numeric_inputs = (
        matric_suction_psi_kpa,
        slope_beta_deg,
        alpha_vg_inv_kpa,
        van_genuchten_n,
        unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3,
        cohesion_c_eff_kpa,
        friction_phi_eff_deg,
        slip_depth_z_m,
    )
    if not all(math.isfinite(v) for v in numeric_inputs):
        raise ValueError("All unsaturated soil and slope parameters must be finite numbers")
    if matric_suction_psi_kpa < 0.0:
        raise ValueError("Matric suction psi = u_a - u_w must be >= 0 kPa in the unsaturated zone")
    if alpha_vg_inv_kpa <= 0.0:
        raise ValueError("van Genuchten alpha_vg (kPa^-1) must be strictly positive")
    if van_genuchten_n <= 1.0:
        raise ValueError("van Genuchten exponent n must be > 1.0 so m = 1 - 1/n lies in (0, 1)")
    if not (0.0 < slope_beta_deg < 90.0):
        raise ValueError("Slope inclination beta (deg) must lie in (0, 90)")
    if not (0.0 < friction_phi_eff_deg < 90.0):
        raise ValueError("Drained friction angle phi' (deg) must lie in (0, 90)")
    if unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3 <= 0.0 or slip_depth_z_m <= 0.0:
        raise ValueError("Unit weight gamma and slip-plane depth z must be strictly positive")
    if cohesion_c_eff_kpa < 0.0:
        raise ValueError("Effective cohesion c' must be non-negative")

    m = 1.0 - (1.0 / van_genuchten_n)
    if matric_suction_psi_kpa == 0.0:
        effective_sat = 1.0
        rel_perm_kr = 1.0
        suction_stress_kpa = 0.0
    else:
        scaled = alpha_vg_inv_kpa * matric_suction_psi_kpa
        effective_sat = (1.0 + scaled ** van_genuchten_n) ** (-m)
        u = min(1.0, max(0.0, effective_sat ** (1.0 / m)))
        if u >= 1.0:
            inner = 1.0
        elif u <= 0.0:
            inner = 0.0
        else:
            inner = -math.expm1(m * math.log1p(-u))
        rel_perm_kr = math.sqrt(effective_sat) * (inner * inner)
        chi_eff = 1.0 if planted_defect else effective_sat
        suction_stress_kpa = -chi_eff * matric_suction_psi_kpa

    beta_rad = math.radians(slope_beta_deg)
    phi_rad = math.radians(friction_phi_eff_deg)
    tan_beta = math.tan(beta_rad)
    tan_phi = math.tan(phi_rad)
    denom_shear = unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3 * slip_depth_z_m * math.sin(beta_rad) * math.cos(beta_rad)
    sigma_n_net_kpa = unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3 * slip_depth_z_m * (math.cos(beta_rad) ** 2)
    sigma_n_eff_kpa = sigma_n_net_kpa - suction_stress_kpa
    shear_tau_kpa = denom_shear

    factor_of_safety = (tan_phi / tan_beta) + (
        (cohesion_c_eff_kpa - suction_stress_kpa * tan_phi) / denom_shear
    )

    if factor_of_safety < 1.0:
        regime = "BELOW LIMIT EQUILIBRIUM (FS < 1.0, model)"
    elif factor_of_safety < 1.2:
        regime = "MARGINAL EQUILIBRIUM (1.0 <= FS < 1.2, model)"
    else:
        regime = "ABOVE LIMIT EQUILIBRIUM (FS >= 1.2, model)"

    return {
        "folio": "E.14",
        "effective_saturation_se": round(effective_sat, 3),
        "relative_conductivity_kr": round(rel_perm_kr, 4),
        "suction_stress_kpa": round(suction_stress_kpa, 2),
        "effective_normal_stress_kpa": round(sigma_n_eff_kpa, 2),
        "shear_demand_kpa": round(shear_tau_kpa, 2),
        "factor_of_safety": round(factor_of_safety, 3),
        "slope_pass": factor_of_safety >= 1.0,
        "regime": regime,
    }


def _check_bounds_rejections() -> bool:
    invalid_cases = (
        dict(matric_suction_psi_kpa=-1.0),
        dict(van_genuchten_n=1.0),
        dict(van_genuchten_n=0.8),
        dict(alpha_vg_inv_kpa=0.0),
        dict(slope_beta_deg=0.0),
        dict(slope_beta_deg=90.0),
        dict(friction_phi_eff_deg=0.0),
        dict(unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3=0.0),
        dict(slip_depth_z_m=-0.5),
        dict(cohesion_c_eff_kpa=-1.0),
        dict(matric_suction_psi_kpa=float("nan")),
    )
    for kw in invalid_cases:
        try:
            evaluate_unsaturated_slope(**kw)
            return False
        except ValueError:
            pass
    return True


def run_verification(planted_defect: bool = False) -> dict[str, Any]:
    nominal = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(planted_defect=planted_defect)
    saturated = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(matric_suction_psi_kpa=0.0, planted_defect=planted_defect)
    near_onset = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(matric_suction_psi_kpa=2.0, planted_defect=planted_defect)
    dry_loam = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(matric_suction_psi_kpa=120.0, planted_defect=planted_defect)
    clean_sand_peak = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(
        matric_suction_psi_kpa=7.964,
        slope_beta_deg=36.0,
        alpha_vg_inv_kpa=0.145,
        van_genuchten_n=2.68,
        unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3=18.0,
        cohesion_c_eff_kpa=0.0,
        friction_phi_eff_deg=32.0,
        slip_depth_z_m=2.0,
        planted_defect=planted_defect,
    )
    clean_sand_dry = evaluate_unsaturated_slope(
        matric_suction_psi_kpa=40.0,
        slope_beta_deg=36.0,
        alpha_vg_inv_kpa=0.145,
        van_genuchten_n=2.68,
        unit_weight_gamma_kn_m3=18.0,
        cohesion_c_eff_kpa=0.0,
        friction_phi_eff_deg=32.0,
        slip_depth_z_m=2.0,
        planted_defect=planted_defect,
    )

    checks = {
        "nominal_se_0_904": nominal["effective_saturation_se"] == 0.904,
        "nominal_kr_0_1662": nominal["relative_conductivity_kr"] == 0.1662,
        "nominal_suction_stress_neg_21_69": nominal["suction_stress_kpa"] == -21.69,
        "nominal_fs_1_557": nominal["factor_of_safety"] == 1.557 and nominal["slope_pass"],
        "saturated_psi_0_kr_1_and_unstable": (
            saturated["effective_saturation_se"] == 1.0
            and saturated["relative_conductivity_kr"] == 1.0
            and saturated["suction_stress_kpa"] == 0.0
            and saturated["factor_of_safety"] == 0.902
            and (not saturated["slope_pass"])
        ),
        "unsaturated_wetting_collapse_at_psi_2": (
            near_onset["suction_stress_kpa"] == -2.0
            and near_onset["factor_of_safety"] == 0.962
            and (not near_onset["slope_pass"])
        ),
        "silty_loam_monotonic_suction_n_1_60": abs(dry_loam["suction_stress_kpa"]) > abs(nominal["suction_stress_kpa"]),
        "clean_sand_non_monotonic_peak_7_96kpa": (
            abs(clean_sand_peak["suction_stress_kpa"]) > abs(clean_sand_dry["suction_stress_kpa"])
        ),
        "clean_sand_dry_40kpa_desaturated_unstable": (
            clean_sand_dry["effective_saturation_se"] == 0.052
            and clean_sand_dry["suction_stress_kpa"] == -2.08
            and clean_sand_dry["factor_of_safety"] == 0.936
            and (not clean_sand_dry["slope_pass"])
        ),
        "invalid_bounds_raise_value_error": _check_bounds_rejections(),
    }
    ok = all(checks.values())
    return {
        "status": "PASS" if ok else "FAIL",
        "folio_id": "E.14",
        "planted_defect": planted_defect,
        "nominal": nominal,
        "saturated": saturated,
        "near_onset_psi_2": near_onset,
        "clean_sand_peak": clean_sand_peak,
        "clean_sand_dry_40kpa": clean_sand_dry,
        "checks": checks,
    }
