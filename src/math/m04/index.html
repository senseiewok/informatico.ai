Act I — The Human Story & Historical Turning Point (M.04)

In additive combinatorics, a set A in F_2^n has small doubling if |A + A| <= K|A| for a modest constant K. The Polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa conjecture, attributed to Katalin Marton and widely known under that name, asks whether such a set must be covered by a number of cosets of a subspace that is only polynomial in K. In November 2023, Timothy Gowers, Ben Green, Freddie Manners and Terence Tao posted a proof in characteristic 2 (arXiv:2311.05762; Annals of Mathematics 2025, DOI 10.4007/annals.2025.201.2.5).

In Plain Words · Extra-Easy Explanation

In plain words, this puzzle asks how closely a messy collection of points must resemble a tidy, organized grid. Imagine tossing a handful of coins onto a blanket. If adding their positions together produces very few new positions, the coins must secretly share a hidden alignment. The conjecture says that such a set can be covered by a modest number of tidy frames, where "modest" means a polynomial in the doubling constant. This folio makes no claim about data compression, streaming or encryption.

Gowers, Green, Manners and Tao establish the conjecture in characteristic 2 using Shannon entropy and an entropic version of Ruzsa distance (Annals of Mathematics 2025, DOI 10.4007/annals.2025.201.2.5 [Scite ↗]). The repository github.com/teorth/pfr holds a Lean 4 formalization project whose README states that its main purpose, formalizing the proof, was completed, followed by a second stage developing consequences. The Equational Theories project (github.com/teorth/equational_theories), launched on 25 September 2024 according to its README, concerns the implications among 4,694 magma laws, that is 4,694 × 4,693 = 22,028,942 ordered pairs. Its README says that accepted implications are verified in Lean and that other claims are tracked as conjectured. This site has not built either project and has not checked how far the second one has progressed.

A blueprint splits a proof into small lemmas that different people can formalize independently, with a dependency graph showing what remains. The README of the PFR project links its blueprint and describes the formalization as completed. Whether this way of working carries over to other proofs is not something the folio assesses.
